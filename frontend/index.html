How a session works

From setup to scorecard in three steps.

1

Pick your setup

Choose the language you're practicing, your level, and a mode — free talk, a scenario like a restaurant or airport, or tutor mode.

2

Talk out loud

Your AI partner calls you in the browser. It adapts to your level: simple sentences for beginners, natural pace with idioms for advanced speakers.

3

Get graded

The transcript goes to Gemini. You get scores per skill, every mistake corrected with an explanation, and one concrete thing to practice next.

A report card for every conversation

Most apps tell you your streak. We tell you what you actually got wrong.

  • Scores per skillGrammar, vocabulary, fluency and comprehension, 0–100 each.
  • Every mistake, correctedWhat you said, what a native speaker would say, and why.
  • Target-language shareWhat percentage of the conversation you held in the language you're learning.
  • Progress that compoundsAverages, best scores and practice streaks tracked per language.
